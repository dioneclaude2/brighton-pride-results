Overall verdict

Did the campaign work?

Yes — and the ad spend did exactly what it was meant to do: the campaign generated 684,097 views and reached 492,224 accounts across 20 posts (11 organic, 9 boosted) in two weeks, with the 9 boosted posts driving 83% of that volume (569,715 of 684,097 views), including both of the top 2 highest-viewed posts — a clear sign the paid promotion worked. On top of that, the 11 organic-only posts still pulled in a solid 114,382 views (17%) with zero spend behind them, a good baseline to build from. Engagement rate landed at 0.38% of reach — modest, and an easy lever to push higher next time. HK$36,687.39 in spend bought a blended CPM of HK$45.42, with two ad-account line items worth a quick confirm to make the numbers fully clean. Net read: the campaign delivered real reach, largely through paid promotion doing its job — worth budgeting for again, and worth testing whether the organic-only formats scale just as well with a bit of spend behind them.

Performance

Campaign effectiveness

How the campaign performed overall, independent of which team or platform drove it.

Volume

20 posts over 14 days (31 Jul – 13 Aug) generated 684,097 views and 492,224 accounts reached — an average of 34,205 views and 24,611 reach per post. Real volume for a two-week window.

Repeat exposure

684,097 views against 492,224 accounts reached works out to 1.39 views per person reached — the average reached account saw campaign content more than once.

Engagement

1,856 total engagement actions (likes, comments, saves, shares) against 492,224 reach — a 0.38% engagement rate. The campaign leaned more on breadth of reach than depth of engagement per viewer.

Concentration

The top 2 posts (of 20) drove 478,617 views — 70% of the campaign total. Both were boosted, so that concentration is partly a budget story — worth confirming how much of it was creative vs. spend before repeating the format.

Organic strength

The 11 organic posts (no paid support) generated 114,382 views on their own — real organic traction independent of media spend.

Extended reach

Content Forge's Facebook cross-posting added 35,960 views not counted in the Instagram totals above — real reach the headline number doesn't capture.

Bottom line

Reading this together

Across all three teams, the campaign generated 684,097 organic views, 492,224 accounts reached, and 1,856 total engagements, on HK$36,687.39 in matched ad spend. Ad budget was concentrated on one team (92%), which shapes the raw totals — more spend behind a team's posts means more views and reach, independent of the content itself. Reading engagement rate (per person reached) alongside the raw totals gives a fuller picture of how each team's content landed with the audience it reached. For next time: closing the two flagged gaps in the ad-spend table (the tentative and unmatched rows) would make budget attribution cleaner across the board — Team Nancy's smaller post count reflects its supporting role on this campaign rather than a gap to close.

Methodology — how this report was built (11 notes)
  1. Organic metrics (Views, Reach, Likes, Comments, Saves, Shares) were pulled directly from each post's native Instagram Insights panel (admin view on hellonancy_official), not estimated.
  2. For Reels, Instagram's own disclaimer states native Views EXCLUDE plays delivered as an ad, so the Views figure in Lauren's Team and most Content Forge rows is organic-only, separate from paid Ad Views.
  3. For image/carousel posts, Instagram's disclaimer differs and does NOT state ad plays are excluded — so for those posts (e.g. Spreading the love at Brighton Pride), the Views figure may already include some ad-driven views. True organic-only views for that post are likely closer to (Views − Ad Views).
  4. Boosted vs Organic status was determined ONLY by matching each post's caption text against the 12 ad names in the CSV — not assumed. Instagram's own "Ad" section on each post was checked but found to appear on every post regardless of ad history, so it was not usable as a signal.
  5. "Brighton Pride with the Nancy team" appears TWICE in the ad CSV with different spend/views. Only one post (Lauren's Team, DbgHLVGjTMr) has a caption starting with this exact phrase, so both ad line items are tentatively attributed to it — flagged as unconfirmed rather than split arbitrarily.
  6. Two ads ("Weather forecast for Brighton Pride", HK$2,748.09 spend; "More color, more laughter, more freedom, more pleasure", HK$6.40 spend) have no matching caption in any of the 21 links provided across all 3 teams. Not matched to any post — flagged as unresolved rather than guessed.
  7. Content Forge link #1 (DbdAn7Sshf3) was submitted twice in the original list — counted once.
  8. Several Content Forge posts are cross-posted to Facebook; their FB Views/Reactions/Comments are recorded separately and are NOT included in the IG Views/Likes/Comments totals.
  9. Dates for posts without an exact date shown (Instagram displayed "Xd"/"Xw" instead of a calendar date) were computed from that relative timestamp against Aug 18, 2026 — treat as approximate.
  10. Currency: all ad spend figures are in HKD as reported in the source CSV.
